The Tune Up

Five stages, modeled on the Apple Steps of Service

A Tune Up is not generic QA and it is not a promise of distribution. It is a customer-journey readiness review: a first-time visitor with thirty seconds of patience per screen clicks through your flows, submits your forms, typos, hits back mid-flow — and scores each stage 1–5 against a public rubric.

StageThe question it answers
ApproachDo the first five seconds tell me what this is and why I'd care?
ProbeDoes onboarding learn what I need, or dump me somewhere empty?
PresentCan I accomplish the core thing? How many steps, how many dead ends?
ListenWhat happens when I typo, submit blanks, or hit back mid-flow?
EndIs there a confirmation, a farewell, a reason to return — or does the journey just stop?
The Report

Every score cites a moment you can check

The output is a service report, not a takedown. Every score below 4 ships with a concrete observation the builder can fix, and every claim cites a step number and a screenshot from the run.

How it works

A register, an extension, and a way in

For people browsing

  • A browser extension appraises app listings in place, starting with Show HN.
  • Apps that passed a Tune Up wear a laurel. Unknowns get scored against cheap, open heuristics.
  • You set the strictness — hide, dim, or just label. Your personal trust list beats every other signal, always.

For builders

  • Request a Tune Up: one form, your URL, and the core journey a first-time customer should complete.
  • Pass — no stage below 3, total ≥ 18/25 — and every Tribute install stops flagging you within a day.
  • Fail, and you get the exact journey breaks, cited. Fix them and resubmit; history stays visible.
Principles

Verdicts can't be bought

01

The rubric is open data. Scoring criteria live in versioned markdown; editing the rubric is a pull request, and every report is reproducible against the version that scored it.

02

Failed reports stay public. Resubmission is a repair path, not an eraser. Trust reflects the latest reviewed journey, with history kept visible.

03

The reviewer operates inside hard boundaries. No payments, no attacks, one test account at most, no mess left behind. The Tune Up is a service the builder wants.

04

The repo is the database. Every verdict has blame, history, and a review trail. Misclassified? File an issue — every fix ships to everyone.

Status

Pre-alpha: a walking skeleton

Everything is thin but connected end to end — rubric, register, heuristics, Tune Up pipeline, extension. The inaugural cohort of five apps is being drawn live from Show HN to stress-test the rubric across very different journey shapes. Inspired by Knockoff, which does this for pseudo-brands on Amazon.
