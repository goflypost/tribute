# Approach

**Question:** Do the first five seconds tell me what this is and why I'd care?

## Mission

Land on the page. Do not click anything. You may scroll once. Using only what
is visible, state: (1) what this app is, (2) who it's for, (3) what you'd do
first. Note whether the page looks finished — real favicon, real copy, no
placeholder text, no broken layout.

Max 5 actions.

## Scoring anchors

- **5** — Instantly legible: a headline that says what it does, a visible next
  step, finished visual details (favicon, OG-quality copy, no placeholders).
  You could explain the product to someone else after five seconds.
- **4** — Clear within one scroll. Minor rough edges (generic tagline, weak
  hierarchy) but no confusion about what it is.
- **3** — You can work it out, but it takes effort: vague headline, feature
  list before value proposition, or the page leans on a screenshot to explain
  itself.
- **2** — Genuinely unclear what the product is or who it's for. Placeholder
  or template copy visible. Default framework title or favicon.
- **1** — Blank page, broken render, lorem ipsum, or the page is actively
  misleading about what the product does.
