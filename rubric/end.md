# End

**Question:** Is there a confirmation, a farewell, a reason to return — or does the journey just stop?

## Mission

Complete (or reach the natural end of) the core flow, then observe what the
app does with the moment: is there a confirmation of what just happened, a
clear record you can return to, a suggested next step, a way to leave
gracefully? Check what happens if you close the tab and come back: is your
work still there? Is there any signal the app wants a relationship rather than
a transaction?

Max 15 actions.

## Scoring anchors

- **5** — A fond farewell: explicit confirmation of the outcome, durable
  evidence of your work, a natural reason to return, and a return visit that
  picks up where you left off.
- **4** — Clean ending with one missing beat: confirmation but no next step,
  or state persists but nothing acknowledges your return.
- **3** — The flow ends without ceremony: the action completed and you can
  tell, but the app offers nothing further. Acceptable for micro-utilities.
- **2** — Ambiguous ending: no confirmation that the core action succeeded,
  or a return visit loses your work without warning.
- **1** — The journey just stops: dead-end final screen, lost state, or no
  way to tell whether anything happened at all.
